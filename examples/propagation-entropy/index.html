Propagation & entropy

initializeWfcMaps with a seed, then propagateAllCollapsed to shrink superpositions without the full WFC loop. Cell color = remaining variant count (entropy); collapsed cells show A, B, or C.

A may neighbor A or C; B may neighbor B or C; C is a shared edge type and may neighbor any of the three (think grass / water / shore). The center is seeded A, so propagation removes pure B next to A and leaves mixed superpositions where both biomes could still reach. Serve repo root after npm run build; open /examples/propagation-entropy/.
